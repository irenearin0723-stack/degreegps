## How to read the course-by-course table

- **R — registration:** verified applicable access conditions/periods and a usable canonical policy are absent. Confirm whether restrictions exist; do not assume every possible restriction applies. All 64 have R, even when partial facts already exist.
- **K — existing knowledge not encoded:** section major splits, non-major descriptions or faculty conditions are in repository notes. Preserve/import those facts first; verify only unresolved scope/details.
- **G — grades:** catalog thresholds are present; missing reviewed **student** grade evidence and API plumbing cannot be solved by recopying the catalog.
- **C — concurrency:** existing verified permission is not wired through the planner context.
- **O — offerings:** no positive evidence for that exact quarter. Unknown does not mean not offered. Confirmed listings win over projections for the same term.
- **V — variable credits:** actual chosen credits are unknown; not an independent blocker in the current course-count planner, but needed before credit-load/degree calculations.
- **X — external dependency:** the prerequisite ID is already known and accepted in completed history. Its own schedulable record is absent; only collect that record if the MVP must plan it rather than assume it completed.
- **C snapshot / P tentative / U** = confirmed snapshot / departmental projection / unknown. W27, Sp27 and Su27 mean Winter, Spring and Summer **2027**. Offering source links are per course/term; title links go to that course's stored official catalog URL. Stored catalog/prerequisite and offering check dates are 2026-10-17; these are repository provenance, not rechecks in this task. Autumn 2026 projections remain stored but are outside this future three-quarter horizon.
- Priorities **A1–A8** are the eight first-pass MVP candidates; **B1–B4** are four useful extensions. **Later** means defer verification for this MVP, not academically unimportant or permanently blocked.

No specific student was supplied. Therefore the table reports structural blockers plus conditional student prerequisites, rather than claiming every student lacks each listed prerequisite. Gate order is prerequisite → exact-term offering → major match → unresolved registration ([planner](../lib/planner.ts#L59)). A later quarter may overwrite a warning's earlier reason; the table lists independent blockers instead of copying only the last warning.

“Verified” here means repository evidence has an official source/check date and passes the application schema. It does not certify every current UW policy, guarantee that the notes are exhaustive, or establish a student's approval. The catalog source and the term-specific source have separate scopes.

### 2. Present but not sufficient or not independently verified

- `Restr` flags: the existence of a restriction is recorded, but the permitted population, applicable periods and complete SLN details were not retrieved. Metadata records MyPlan/SLN access failures; there is no reason to request credentials in this task.
- Projected offerings: valid tentative evidence, not confirmed offerings or seat availability. A projection is not “bad data”; correctly labeled tentative assignments may use it after other eligibility checks.
- Candidate lecture/quiz parent combinations and final-exam notes are not independently verified for registration. They matter to a future section scheduler, not to quarter-only recommendations.
- Raw section open/enrolled/capacity values are dated snapshots, not live seats. No fixed seat or registration guarantee may be derived from them.
- Category and skill labels are team mappings, not official degree allocation or proof a student acquired those skills.

### 3. Genuinely missing evidence or student input

- A complete applicable registration-policy decision for each candidate and exact term: permitted program/major, standing **if actually required**, period restrictions, approval/add-code **if applicable**, and evidence identifying the scope. “No restriction” needs affirmative verification; a blank flag is insufficient.
- Confirmation of any required student-specific faculty approval, placement, or permission. Knowing the policy is not knowing that the student satisfies it.
- Reviewed numeric student grades for grade-dependent prerequisites. The catalog thresholds are not missing. The current report diagnoses the inability to supply the student's evidence; it does not contain or request real transcript data.
- Positive offering evidence for the unknown course/term cells in the table. These cells can stay unknown outside the selected MVP path; do not research all 59 unknown Summer records just to fill a report.
- Actual chosen credits for variable-credit workloads. The catalog ranges already exist. Do not choose arbitrary credits to make planning appear complete.
- External courses' own schedulable records only if those courses need to be recommended. Their IDs and their role in existing prerequisite rules are already known.
